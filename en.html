Method

One important decision — as a small research project, not a 3 a.m. anxiety spiral

  1. 1

    Framing the question

    “Something is off” becomes one question — with boundaries and a deadline.

  2. 2

    Map of options

    Every path on one map — including the unrealistic and the embarrassing ones.

  3. 3

    Versions of the future

    Each serious option written as a concrete Tuesday two years from now.

  4. 4

    Testing

    Versions become hypotheses you can test within a week or two.

  5. 5

    Field

    Seven days of short notes: data about yourself, not opinions.

  6. 6

    Decision

    A document with a signature and a review date — instead of a “happy ending”.

Write in pencil while you think; in ink — what has become data.
